A responsibility describes what an object knows or does. A collaborator is another object it needs to perform its work.
Member
A Member knows its member ID and remembers its current loan. It can answer whether it already has a loan and store a reference to a newly created loan.
Its collaborator is Loan, because the member’s current borrowing record is a Loan object.
Book
A Book knows the title and provides that information when needed.
For this exercise, it needs no collaborator to provide its title.
BookCopy
A BookCopy knows its copy ID, the Book it belongs to and whether it is available. It checks its own availability before accepting borrowing. After acceptance, it marks itself unavailable.
Its collaborator is Book, which holds the title information. Checking availability uses BookCopy’s own state.
Loan
A Loan knows the borrowing member, the physical copy, the borrowing date, the due date and the loan status. It represents a successful borrowing relationship.
Its collaborators are Member and BookCopy, because each Loan connects one member with one physical copy.
LibraryDesk
LibraryDesk coordinates the borrowing request. It asks whether the member already has a loan, asks the copy to accept borrowing, creates a Loan after acceptance and records that loan for the member.
Its collaborators are Member, BookCopy and Loan.
The main ownership decision is: BookCopy controls availability, Member remembers its current loan, and LibraryDesk coordinates their work.
